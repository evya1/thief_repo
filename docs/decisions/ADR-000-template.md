# ADR-000 — Decision title

Use an ADR only for a sufficiently important and durable technical design decision. Official-input receipt belongs in the Input Register, product/requirement changes belong in a Change Request, and execution work belongs in a task.

## Context

Describe the durable architecture question, constraints, and evidence. Distinguish source requirements from derived design choices; do not change product scope here.

## Decision

State the chosen design precisely.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not chosen.

## Consequences

Record positive, negative, migration, interoperability, security, and verification effects.

## Validation

List tests, probes, or review gates that demonstrate the decision works.
